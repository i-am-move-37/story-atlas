How this works
  • These are the 40 works you labeled from a blank page. Each comes with a short list of genres. Some are yours, some came from the labeling methods, and some are decoys nobody suggested. Nothing shows which is which.
  • Star the genres that best describe the work: at least one, at most three. Think of what you'd say to tell someone what kind of story it is.
  • Mark Wrong only when a genre plainly doesn't fit. If someone could defend it, leave it alone.
  • Leave the rest unmarked. Unmarked means "fits more or less" and counts neither for nor against anything.
  • Judge the work itself, not just the summary, as before.
