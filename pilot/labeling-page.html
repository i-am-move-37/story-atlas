How to label
  • Label the work itself, not just this summary. Use what you know, and look the work up when you haven't seen or read it. The Wikipedia link is on each work.
  • Pick the finest label that applies. A subgenre counts for its parent genre too, so you don't need to pick both.
  • Core means the work plainly belongs to that tradition. Borderline means a defensible stretch, like calling Brick a neo-western.
  • A mix of two genres is two labels. A science fiction western gets science fiction and western. Romantic comedy is the one exception.
  • Plain dramas and comedies may fit nothing. Tick "No genre in the list fits" rather than forcing a label.
  • The first 50 works start from a blank page. The rest arrive with suggested labels to confirm, change or remove.
